# F33 — LIVING SPACES INTELLIGENCE

## Architecture Decision

**Crear `FacilityReservation` como modelo y tools dedicadas** — NO reutilizar court bookings de sports.

Razón: Las reservas de facilities tienen semántica diferente a canchas deportivas (duración variable, coordinación con eventos, approval flows distintos). Un modelo dedicado nos da: booking real desde el dashboard, detección de conflictos con events/maintenance, datos de utilización basados en reservas reales (no snapshots), y cross-domain con sports (espacios compartidos) y events (venue availability). Pattern: 2 tools nuevas (`create_facility_reservation` HITL + `get_facility_reservations` AUTO), siguiendo el mismo patrón que `create_reservation` en sports.

**Total tools F33: 7 existentes + 2 reservation + 4 intelligence = 13 tools** (alineado con sports 11, communications 14).

---

## La Propuesta: Living Spaces Intelligence

No construimos un dashboard de facilities. Construimos un sistema que hace que las instalaciones del club **respiren con inteligencia** — que sepa qué espacios están vivos, cuáles necesitan atención, cuáles generan valor, y qué va a pasar mañana.

### El concepto

```
LIVING SPACES INTELLIGENCE
│
├── VER: Live Occupancy Board — cada espacio tiene un pulso
├── ENTENDER: 9-dimension analysis — utilization, maintenance, revenue, compliance...
├── PREDECIR: Predictive Maintenance Scoring — formula compuesta (M2)
├── OPTIMIZAR: Space ROI — revenue por m² por facility (M3)
├── ACTUAR: 6 acciones ejecutables con one-click + HITL
└── APRENDER: Reaction system + cross-domain feedback loop
```

### Lo que nadie tiene en clubes de CR

| Feature | Software tradicional | Castillo F33 |
|---------|---------------------|--------------|
| Ver espacios | Lista de nombres | Live Occupancy Board con pulso en tiempo real |
| Mantenimiento | Calendario reactivo | Predictive Maintenance Scoring (condition × age × usage × weather) |
| Valor de espacios | No existe | Space ROI: revenue / (maintenance + opportunity cost) por m² |
| Patrones | No existe | Heatmap 7×16: cuándo está lleno cada espacio |
| Inteligencia | Tablas | 9 dimensions + advisor con 12 reglas + cross-domain signals |
| Acciones | Forms manuales | 6 acciones one-click con approval intelligence |

## Backend Architecture

### 4 Intelligence Tools (nuevas)

| # | Tool | Approval | Propósito |
|---|------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | `get_facility_analytics` | AUTO | 9-dimension analysis: Health, Utilization, Maintenance Intel, Space Optimization, Revenue, Compliance, Event Readiness, Cross-Domain, Prediction |
| 2 | `get_amenity_utilization` | AUTO | Granular utilization: per-facility %, peak hours, time series 7d/30d, hourly breakdown, forecast |
| 3 | `get_space_optimization` | AUTO | Cross-domain suggestions: underutilized, conflicting, expansion candidates, ROI per space |
| 4 | `get_facility_health` | AUTO | Composite health: 5 factors (utilization 25%, maintenance 25%, inspection 20%, satisfaction 15%, cost_efficiency 15%), grade A-F, trend |

### 2 Reservation Tools (nuevas)

| # | Tool | Approval | Propósito |
|---|------|----------|-----------|
| 5 | `create_facility_reservation` | HITL/SEMI | Booking con conflict detection. HITL para premium spaces (ballroom, restaurant), SEMI para standard |
| 6 | `get_facility_reservations` | AUTO | Listar reservas por facility/date range |

### 4 Response Models (nuevas)

| Model | Fields clave |
|-------|-------------|
| `FacilityAnalyticsResponse` | dimensions[9], overall_score, cross_domain_signals[] |
| `AmenityUtilizationItem` | facility_id, utilization_pct, current_occupancy, capacity, peak_hours[], trend, forecast_pct, time_series[] |
| `SpaceOptimizationSuggestion` | facility_id, suggestion_type, current_use, suggested_use, estimated_impact, priority, roi_estimate |
| `FacilityReservation` | id, facility_id, facility_name, family_id, family_name, date, start_time, end_time, status, guest_count, purpose, notes |

### 6 Repo Analytics Methods (nuevas)

| Method | Cómputo |
|--------|---------|
| `get_facility_analytics()` | Agrega: occupancy, maintenance, inspections, setups → 9 dimensions |
| `get_amenity_utilization()` | Per-facility: capacity vs current, peak detection, time series |
| `get_space_optimization()` | Cross-domain: underutilized (util<20% 30d), revenue vs cost, expansion |
| `get_facility_health()` | 5-factor weighted score con trend calculation |
| `create_reservation()` | CRUD reservation + conflict check |
| `list_reservations()` | Filtros por facility/date/status |

## Advisor Architecture: `useFacilitiesAdvisor`

### 12 Rules × 5 Layers

| # | Rule | Condition | Impact | Layer | Action |
|---|------|-----------|--------|-------|--------|
| 1 | utilization-critical | util < 30% avg 7d | HIGH | Diagnostic | Cerrar para maint o reasignar |
| 2 | utilization-excellent | util > 85% | LOW | Optimization | Expandir horario + pricing premium |
| 3 | maintenance-overdue | overdue_count > 0 | HIGH | Diagnostic | Programar inmediatamente |
| 4 | maintenance-cost-spike | cost > 2× trailing avg | MEDIUM | Strategy | Auditar proveedores + presupuesto |
| 5 | inspection-overdue | next_inspection < today | MEDIUM | Compliance | Programar inspección |
| 6 | inspection-fail-rate | fail_rate > 20% | HIGH | Diagnostic | Revisar estándares + capacitación |
| 7 | space-underutilized | util < 20% por 30d | MEDIUM | Innovation | Evaluar reasignación de uso |
| 8 | booking-conflict | overlapping reservations | HIGH | Execution | Resolver conflictos + policy |
| 9 | revenue-negative-roi | maint_cost > revenue 90d | HIGH | Strategy | Evaluar cierre/renovación |
| 10 | seasonal-demand-shift | seasonal_change imminent | MEDIUM | Prediction | Preparar transición |
| 11 | cross-domain-event-surge | high_event_demand + low_venue | MEDIUM | Innovation | Expandir venues disponibles |
| 12 | cross-domain-sports-overflow | sports_capacity > 90% | MEDIUM | Innovation | Redirect a facilities alternativas |

### 6 Executable Actions

| Insight | Tool | Server | Approval |
|---------|------|--------|----------|
| maint-schedule | `schedule_maintenance` | `facility_management` | HITL |
| issue-report | `report_facility_issue` | `facility_management` | AUTO |
| inspection-create | `manage_inspections` (create) | `facility_management` | AUTO |
| setup-coordinate | `coordinate_event_setup` | `facility_management` | HITL |
| facility-update | `manage_facilities` (update_status) | `facility_management` | AUTO |
| reservation-create | `create_facility_reservation` | `facility_management` | SEMI |

## Cross-Domain Signals

### Inbound (otros dominios → facilities)

| Source | Signal | Impact |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Sports | Court utilization overflow | Redirect a facilities alternativas |
| Events | Event demand surge | Venue availability alert |
| Finance | Maintenance budget exceeded | Cost optimization pressure |
| Communications | Member complaints sobre facilities | Satisfaction signal |
| Wellness | Spa/gym demand patterns | Capacity planning |

### Outbound (facilities → otros dominios)

| Target | Signal | Impact |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Events | Venue availability | "Ballroom available next Saturday" |
| Sports | Shared space conditions | "Court 3 closed for maintenance" |
| Communications | Closure notifications | "Gym cerrado 15-16 junio" |
| Finance | Maintenance costs | "₡2.3M en mantenimiento Q2" |
